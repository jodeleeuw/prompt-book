ACT ONE

(A lamp room. Night. The beam turns steadily.)

KEEPER: You're late.

VISITOR
The road washed out at the bridge. I walked the last two miles.

KEEPER: Nobody walks the last two miles.

VISITOR: I did.

KEEPER
(after a pause)
Then you'll want the chair by the stove. It's the only warm thing left
in this building, and I'm including myself in that.

VISITOR: You've been up here how long?

KEEPER: Long enough that the question stopped meaning anything.

(The beam passes across both their faces.)

VISITOR: They're shutting it down. That's what I came to tell you.

KEEPER: I know what you came to tell me.

---

ACT TWO

VISITOR: You knew?

KEEPER
The supply boat brings letters, and letters bring news, and news up here
travels exactly as fast as the boat. (beat) I've known since March.

VISITOR: And you said nothing.

KEEPER: To whom?

VISITOR: To me. To anyone.

KEEPER
You've been here four hours. I've been here nineteen years. Forgive me
for not rushing.

VISITOR: I'm sorry.

KEEPER: Don't be. It's a light. It did what lights do.

(A long silence. The beam turns.)

VISITOR: And what will you do?

KEEPER: Go somewhere flat. Somewhere the dark stays where you put it.
